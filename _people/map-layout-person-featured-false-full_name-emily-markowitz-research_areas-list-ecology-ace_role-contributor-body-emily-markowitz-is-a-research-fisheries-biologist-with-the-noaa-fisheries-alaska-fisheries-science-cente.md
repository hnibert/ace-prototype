Emily Markowitz is a Research Fisheries Biologist with the NOAA Fisheries' Alaska Fisheries Science Center (AFSC) Bering Sea survey team in Seattle, WA. In this role, she participates in research surveys, conducts statistical analyses, and develops data products to better understand the marine ecosystems of Alaska.
