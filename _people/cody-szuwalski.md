I write stock assessments and sit on the Plan Team that provides catch advice for Bering Sea and Aleutian Islands crab. I use quantitative methods to identify drivers of change in ecological systems and then use simulation to project plausible future scenarios to evaluate potential management responses under uncertainty. Sometimes I am also an ‘ecological detective’, like the time billions of snow crab disappeared from the Bering Sea and we didn’t know why.
