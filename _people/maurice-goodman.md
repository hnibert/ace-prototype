Maurice is a quantitative ecologist whose research aims to describe, explain, and predict change in marine ecosystems. In particular, he is interested in the processes that determine the spatial distributions (ranges) of marine species and the emergent ecological and management implications of species range shifts. His projects include (1) modeling and projecting Eastern Bering Sea (EBS) species range shifts, (2) combining projected EBS range shifts with multispecies population models to characterize management implications of climate-driven range shifts, (3) assessing approaches to tracking spatial shifts at the scale of ecological communities, and (4) adapting an ecosystem model (ecostate) to estimate environmental effects on EBS community dynamics.
