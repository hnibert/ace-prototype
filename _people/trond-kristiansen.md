Trond Kristiansen’s work focuses on assessing future changes to fisheries in Arctic and sub-Arctic regions. For this work, he will use data on temperature, oxygen, and pH across multiple life stages to determine Walleye pollock, Sabelfish, Snow crab, Tanner crab, and Arctic cod current and future habitats. Results from this work can then be used by e.g. fishery managers or scientists.
