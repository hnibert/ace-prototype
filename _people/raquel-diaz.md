I am expanding the multispecies model CEATTLE (Climate-Enhanced Age-based model with Temperature-specific Trophic Linkages and Energetics), developing methods to incorporate predators even when prey-at-age data are limited. Using this enhanced framework, I assess predation mortality on Pacific hake from key predators, including California sea lions, Arrowtooth flounder, Sablefish, and hake cannibalism. By partitioning natural mortality into predation and residual components, my work provides updated estimates of time- and age-varying mortality for Pacific Hake, which may improve population estimates and offer new insights into stock status relative to fishery management reference points.
