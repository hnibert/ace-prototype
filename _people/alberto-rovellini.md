My research focuses on developing and applying ecological models to fisheries management in the North Pacific. My main focus is the continued development of the Atlantis ecosystem model for the Gulf of Alaska. I am applying Atlantis to evaluate the performance of various fisheries management strategies under future climate change in the Gulf of Alaska, with a focus on the use of ecosystem caps.
