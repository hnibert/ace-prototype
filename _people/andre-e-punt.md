My research and that of my lab is focused on developing frameworks that integrate multiple ecosystem and fishery drivers on the population dynamics of fish and invertebrate populations using mathematical and statistical models. I am the UW PI lead for the ACLIM, GOACLIM and CEFI projects where my main role to develop and then apply management strategy evaluation methods to explore the consequences of the management strategies selected for Alaskan crab fisheries. I also develop extended single-species models to understand the effects of environmental drivers on the populations with an emphasis on temperature and pH.
