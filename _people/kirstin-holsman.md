I am a quantitative climate scientist with a creative flair and enthusiasm for developing innovative solutions to complex, dynamic challenges. Driven by a passion for ocean systems, I am dedicated to understanding the drivers of change in marine environments to help decision-makers effectively respond to climate change shocks and long-term impacts. My work integrates science-based advice with meaningful collaboration to create actionable solutions for sustainable marine resource management.

I have a keen interest in coding, open science, analyses, and learning new quantitative methods, I am most proud of my capacity to lead diverse interdisciplinary teams through integrative processes, combining varied expertise, lived experiences, and quantitative methods to co-develop next-generation marine resource management and decision support tools. I have found that collaborations across diverse interdisciplinary teams is both crucial for efficient, effective, and publicly accessible science as well as fundamental to my own personal and professional growth.
