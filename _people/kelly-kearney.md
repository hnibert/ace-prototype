I am a physical scientist at NOAA's Alaska Fisheries Science Center. My research interests center on the processes controlling energy flow through ocean ecosystems, from nutrient cycling and primary production through predator/prey interactions and fisheries harvesting.

Most of my work focuses on the development and use of regional and global earth system models.  This includes development of the Alaska-region ocean/biogeochemical models that are used in the ACLIM and CEFI projects, including the Bering10K ROMS and newly-introduced northeast Pacific (NEP) MOM6 model configurations.
