#Justification for Classification (use a '>-' block scalar indicator for text below)
justification: |
    Northern Fur Seals were selected as a species of concern due to their use as a vital cultural and subsistence resource for Unangan (the Aleut people) of St. Paul Island, Alaska for food, cultural continuity, arts, and crafts (Divine et al., 2021).
    
    The Pribilof Islands in Alaska were once home to about 75% of the world’s northern fur seals, but populations have shown a dramatic unexplained decline in recent decades. And lastly, a total of 286 laaqudan were harvested during the 2020 harvest season (Divine et al;., 2021).
