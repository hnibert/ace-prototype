justification: |
    Norton sound red king crab and recent declines and closures have raised concerns over food sovereignty and well-being for subsistence harvesters within the Norton Sound region. Additionally, as a commercially-targeted species, the small summer commercial fishery is a very high-value fishery. 
    
    In 2021, commercial landings of all king crab in Alaska totaled 6 million pounds and were valued at more than $73 million, according to the NOAA Fisheries commercial fishing landings database.
