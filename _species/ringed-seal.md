#Justification for Classification (use a '>-' block scalar indicator for text below)
justification: |
    Ringed seals have been selected as a species of concern, as they are a vital subsistence resource of coastal Alaska Native communities. Highly-productive hunters commonly distribute marine mammal products to other households through non-commercial networks of sharing and trade, which is key to consider in Alaska's mixed economy. 
    
    "Ringed seals are also hunted for their meat and blubber, whereas their skin, along with spotted seal skins, are also used for crafts and clothes (Burns et al. 1985; Hovelsrud et al. 2008; Ice Seal Committee 2014)." 
    
    Additionally, Tradtional Knowledge that supports the movement of theses species and identification of denning habitat due to the reliance on these species as an important subsistence species (Lindsay et. al, 2023).
