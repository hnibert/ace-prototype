#Justification for Classification (use a '>-' block scalar indicator for text below)
justification: | 
    Bearded seals were selected as a species of concern as they are a vital subsistence resource to coastal Alaska Native communities. In 2003 for Alaskan marine mammals, the majority of the harvest was composed of bearded seals. 
    
    Highly-productive hunters commonly distribute marine mammal products to other households through non-commercial networks of sharing and trade, which is key to consider in Alaska's mixed economy.
