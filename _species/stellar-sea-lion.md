    Stellar sea lions were selected as a species of concern due to their cultural significance in coastal communities including fostering nutrition and connections amongst individuals and communities. 
    
    Highly-productive hunters commonly distribute marine mammal products to other households through non-commercial networks of sharing and trade, which is key to consider in Alaska's mixed economy. During 2004, the estimated subsistence take of sea lions by Alaska Natives was 216 sea lions, with a 95 percent confidence range of between 147 to 335 sea lions (Wolfe et al., 2005).
