#Justification for Classification (use a '>-' block scalar indicator for text below)
justification: |
    Harbor seals were selected as a species of concern due to their cultural significance in coastal communities including fostering nutrition and connections amongst individuals and communities. 

    Highly-productive hunters commonly distribute marine mammal products to other households through non-commercial networks of sharing and trade, which is key to consider in Alaska's mixed economy. During 2004, the estimated subsistence take of harbor seals by Alaska Natives was 1,822 seals, with a 95 percent confidence range of between 1,420 to 2,469 seals.
