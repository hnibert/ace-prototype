#Cultural Significance (use a '>-' block scalar indicator for text below)
cultural_significance: #>-
    An important source of spiritual and physical sustenance for Alaska natives. Salmon provides a significant source of nutrition in an environment where a subsistence way of life is a necessity for remote communities who do not have the same access to store-bought foods as other communities do. In this way, salmon are critical to the food security of these communities (Loring & Gerlach, 2010). While salmon is harvested for subsistence, it is shared locally and widely both within and between communities in a way which strengthens social bonds (Trainor et al. 2021). The sharing of salmon can also help support the adaptive capacity of certain communities in the face of social and ecological changes (Scaggs et al. 2021). Beyond this, salmon has important cultural importance to many Indigenous groups as it. Human-salmon relationships are central to their way of life and contribute to their spiritual, psychological, and emotional wellbeing (Carothers et al. 2021; Amberson et al. 2016).
