summary_lead: >-
  The Arctic is a polar region characterized by extreme cold, seasonal sea ice cover, and unique ecosystems adapted to harsh environmental conditions. The region supports ice-dependent marine species, and is experiencing rapid environmental change due to warming temperatures and loss of sea ice. Fish, marine mammals, and seabirds provide food and sustain cultural traditions for Alaska Native communities. As the climate warms and sea ice declines, some commercial species such as cod, pollock, crab, and halibut are moving farther north, changing marine food webs and the resources available.
