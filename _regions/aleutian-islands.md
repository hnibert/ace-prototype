summary_lead: >-
  The Aleutian Islands form a volcanic island chain that forms a boundary between the Northern Pacific Ocean  and the Alaska Peninsula. This archipelago marks the boundary between the Bering Sea and the North Pacific Ocean and helps shape regional ocean circulation and climate. Volcanoes, earthquakes, and strong ocean currents create diverse habitats on land and in the sea. As ocean conditions change, culturally and economically important fish such as pollock, cod, and mackerel are shifting where they live, affecting fisheries and the communities that depend on them.
