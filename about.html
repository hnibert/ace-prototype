About ACE

Resource managers and decision-makers urgently need tailored information and tools for effective adaptation to climate impacts on Alaska’s ocean ecosystems.

Alaska’s fisheries and marine ecosystems are changing as the climate shifts, bringing new challenges for resource managers, fishing communities, and decision-makers. To navigate these changes, access to reliable climate and ocean data is more important than ever.

The Alaska Climate and Ecosystem (ACE) Dashboard is a new tool designed to help users explore environmental trends and future projections that affect Alaska’s marine resources. By bringing together climate, ocean, and ecosystem data in one place, the ACE Dashboard makes it easier to track changes, anticipate risks, and plan ahead.

Why Climate Information Matters for Adaptive Planning

Adaptive planning is the process of preparing for an uncertain future by using the best available science to guide decisions. As marine ecosystems change, resource managers and fishing communities need flexible strategies that allow them to adjust to new conditions—whether it’s shifting fish stocks, changing ocean temperatures, or increasing storm activity.

The ACE Dashboard supports adaptive planning by providing up-to-date information on key environmental factors, helping users identify trends, evaluate risks, and develop strategies for a more resilient future.

Part of a Larger Effort to Support Climate-Ready Fisheries

The ACE Dashboard is part of NOAA’s Changing Ecosystems and Fisheries Initiative (CEFI), which works to improve our understanding of climate-driven changes in the ocean and provide tools to support sustainable fisheries management and marine conservation. Through CEFI, scientists and decision-makers are developing climate forecasts, scenario-planning tools, and other resources to help communities and industries adapt to a changing environment.

Understanding Future Ocean Conditions with CMIP6 Climate Projections

One of the most powerful features of the ACE Dashboard is its use of CMIP6 (Coupled Model Intercomparison Project Phase 6) climate projections to help users understand and prepare for changing ocean conditions. CMIP6 is an international collaboration that provides state-of-the-art climate model simulations used to assess past, present, and future climate conditions. These projections help researchers and decision-makers evaluate potential climate impacts on Alaska’s ocean ecosystems, such as temperature changes, ocean acidification, and shifting marine species distributions. ACE Dashboard leverages CMIP6 data to offer valuable insights into long-term environmental trends that will shape Alaska’s fisheries and coastal communities.
